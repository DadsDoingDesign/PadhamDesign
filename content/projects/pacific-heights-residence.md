# Pacific Heights Residence

This historic Victorian home in San Francisco's prestigious Pacific Heights neighborhood required a thoughtful renovation that preserved its architectural heritage while introducing modern comfort and functionality.

## Design Approach

We approached this project with a deep respect for the home's original details, carefully restoring ornate moldings, hardwood floors, and stained glass features. These elements were then complemented with clean-lined contemporary furnishings and a curated art collection, creating a harmonious dialogue between past and present.

## Key Features

- Restored original architectural details including crown moldings and fireplace surrounds
- Custom cabinetry and built-ins designed to maximize space while respecting the historic interior
- Smart home technology integrated discreetly throughout
- Contemporary lighting plan that highlights architectural features and art
- Bespoke furniture pieces designed specifically for challenging spaces

"Working with Padham Design was a revelation. They understood our desire to honor the home's history while creating spaces that work for modern family life. The result is both beautiful and functional—we use every room in the house now."
