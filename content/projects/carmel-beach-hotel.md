# Carmel Beach Hotel

The Carmel Beach Hotel project involved a complete renovation of a historic beachfront property, transforming it into a luxury destination that honors its coastal heritage while introducing modern design elements.

## Design Approach

Our approach focused on creating spaces that connect guests with the natural beauty of Carmel's coastline. Each room features large windows that frame ocean views, while the interior palette draws from the surrounding landscape—soft blues, sandy neutrals, and weathered woods.

## Key Features

- Custom millwork throughout using locally sourced cypress
- Hand-selected natural stone surfaces in bathrooms and common areas
- Commissioned artwork from local Carmel artists
- Bespoke lighting fixtures inspired by marine elements
- Sustainable materials and energy-efficient systems
