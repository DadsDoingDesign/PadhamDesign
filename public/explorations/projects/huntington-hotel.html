Huntington Hotel & Nob Hill Spa

San Francisco, CA · 2020
Hospitality Huntington Hotel & Nob Hill Spa · 2020

“San Francisco’s cultural diversity, written into Italianate, Victorian, and Asian motifs.”

Built in 1924 as a 12-story residential hotel, the steel and brick building with Renaissance/Baroque ornamentation was converted to the prominent Huntington Hotel in 1957. The design concept was derived from San Francisco’s cultural diversity. Influences from historic Italianate building interiors, Victorian architecture, and Asian motifs are carried throughout the hotel and spa in the use of furnishings, colors, accessories and art works. The spa, which formally housed the L’Etoile Restaurant, embraces clean lines, practical materials and fine detailing.
