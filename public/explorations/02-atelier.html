About
Carol Padham, Principal

Her design process is client-focused, starting with the client's vision and involving them closely in every step. Her work is known for clean lines and thoughtful integration with both architecture and environment.

Over 25 years of experience combining beauty and practicality. Working extensively in the Bay Area, Carol has collaborated with leading architectural firms on hospitality and residential projects.

— Carol
