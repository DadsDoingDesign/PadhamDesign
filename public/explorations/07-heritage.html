About

Meet the Designer

Carol Padham, Principal

Her design process is client-focused, starting with the client's vision and involving them closely in every step. Carol's designs are known for their clean lines and thoughtful integration with both architecture and environment, ensuring each space is as functional as it is stylish.

Over 25 years of experience combining beauty and practicality. Working extensively in the Bay Area, Carol has collaborated with leading architectural firms on hospitality and residential projects.

— Carol
