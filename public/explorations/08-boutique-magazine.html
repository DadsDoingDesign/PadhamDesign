The Feature

Carmel-by-the-Sea · 2023
Hospitality · Carmel Beach Hotel

An ocean-inspired palette, framed by cypress and Carmel stone.

The hotel's designers, Carol Padham and Phyllis Martin-Vegue, sought inspiration from the pristine white beach, blue-grey hues of the ocean, surrounding cypress trees, and warm tones of Carmel stone — all of which play out throughout the decor.

Custom walnut-grained millwork includes the minibar and headboards running the length of the rooms, each outfitted with woven-back chairs and cafe tables. Interior designers Carol Padham and Marin-Vogue worked with the palette to create custom furnishings for the guest rooms, which also feature Victoria + Albert bath fixtures.

Profile

A practice built around the client.

— On Carol Padham, Principal

Her design process is client-focused, starting with the client's vision and involving them closely in every step. Carol's designs are known for their clean lines and thoughtful integration with both architecture and environment, ensuring each space is as functional as it is stylish.

Over 25 years of experience combining beauty and practicality. Working extensively in the Bay Area, Carol has collaborated with leading architectural firms on hospitality and residential projects.

— Carol Padham
